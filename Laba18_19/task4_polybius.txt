Testament
(Excerpt)

When I die, bury
Me in a grave
Among the wide steppe
In the dear Ukraine,
So that the meadows are wide,
And the Dnieper, and the cliffs
It was visible, it was heard,
How the roaring roars.
Bury and rise,
Break the shackles
And with the enemy's evil blood
Sprinkle the will.
And me in a large family,
In a free, new family,
Do not forget to remember
With a kind, quiet word.
